# Etsy Seller Guide

Complete guide for selling on Etsy — from shop setup to scaling. Covers listing optimization, Etsy SEO, pricing strategy, Star Seller requirements, advertising, and shop analytics for handmade, vintage, and digital product sellers.

## Role and Scope

**This skill:** Entry point for Etsy: diagnoses where the shop stands across the whole selling lifecycle and routes to the specialist Etsy skills.

Part of: Etsy selling, Etsy listings and search, Etsy pricing, traffic and operations. For neighbouring jobs, use the matching skill instead:

- `etsy-shop-setup` — Opening a new Etsy shop: account, policies, payments, first listings and launch checklist.
- `etsy-shop-branding` — Shop identity on Etsy: name, banner, logo, About section, photo style and packaging.
- `etsy-multi-shop` — Deciding on and running more than one Etsy shop.
- `etsy-star-seller` — Reaching and keeping the Etsy Star Seller badge (messages, shipping, reviews).
- `etsy-shop-analytics` — Reading Etsy Stats to find what drives or blocks sales.
- `etsy-seo` — Full SEO audit and scoring of an Etsy listing (title, tags, description) with the analyzer script.
- `etsy-keyword-research` — Finding the search phrases Etsy buyers use, before writing titles and tags.
- `etsy-seo-tags` — Writing the 13 tags and filling attributes of an Etsy listing.
- `etsy-product-description` — Writing the description text of an Etsy listing.
- `etsy-listing-photography` — Photos and video of an Etsy listing.
- `etsy-competitor-analysis` — Studying competing Etsy shops and listings to find gaps.
- `etsy-pricing-strategy` — Setting Etsy prices from costs, Etsy fees and competition.
- `etsy-shipping-strategy` — Etsy shipping profiles, prices, processing times and free shipping.
- `etsy-advertising` — Etsy Ads on Etsy search (budgets, listings, ROAS).
- `etsy-offsite-ads` — Etsy Offsite Ads fees, attribution and the opt-out decision.
- `etsy-social-media-marketing` — Bringing outside traffic to an Etsy shop from Pinterest, Instagram and TikTok.
- `etsy-seasonal-strategy` — Preparing an Etsy shop for holidays and seasonal peaks.
- `etsy-review-strategy` — Getting more honest Etsy reviews and handling bad ones.
- `etsy-custom-orders` — Personalized and made-to-order items on Etsy.
- `etsy-digital-products` — Selling digital downloads on Etsy.
- `etsy-print-on-demand` — Print-on-demand products on Etsy with a production partner.
