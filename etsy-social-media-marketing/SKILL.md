# Etsy Social Media Marketing

Social media marketing for Etsy — Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok strategies to drive shop traffic.

Bring buyers to an Etsy shop from Pinterest, Instagram and TikTok with content the maker can sustain.

## When to Use

- The shop depends only on Etsy search
- Launching a new collection or a seasonal push
- The seller posts on social media but sees no Etsy traffic

## Inputs to Collect

Products and buyer, available time per week, existing accounts and followers, best-selling listings, photo and video assets, Etsy Stats traffic sources.

## Channel Choice

| Channel | Strength for Etsy sellers | Content |
|---------|---------------------------|---------|
| Pinterest | Long-lived search traffic, gift and decor ideas | Vertical pins linking to listings, boards by theme/occasion |
| Instagram | Brand, community, behind the scenes | Reels of the making process, stories, product posts |
| TikTok | Reach and virality | Process videos, packing orders, before/after |

Start with **one** channel that fits the product and the time available.

## Workflow

1. Pick the channel and a weekly rhythm the seller can keep (e.g. 3 posts).
2. Content pillars: making process, product in use, customer stories (with permission), behind the scenes, launches.
3. Link every post to a specific listing or shop section (use Etsy share links; Offsite Ads only apply to Etsy's own ads, not to the seller's posts).
4. Batch-produce content monthly; reuse each video across channels.
5. Measure in Etsy Stats (social traffic, orders) and in the platform analytics; keep what drives visits.

## Output Format

1. Channel recommendation and why
2. 4-week content calendar with post ideas and target listings
3. Profile optimization checklist (bio, link, highlights)
4. KPIs and review date
