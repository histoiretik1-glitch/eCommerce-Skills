The 60-second depuff

Your morning, in four moves

  1. 0:00 → 0:15

    Roll the eyes

    Cold roller from inner corner outward, under each eye. Light pressure.

  2. 0:15 → 0:30

    Open the neck

    Gua sha flat on the skin, sweep down the sides of the neck to the collarbone.

  3. 0:30 → 0:50

    Trace the jaw

  4. 0:50 → 1:00

    Lift the cheeks

    Long edge from nose to temple, then a final sweep down the neck.

Always glide over oil or serum. Results are temporary depuffing and relaxation, not permanent reshaping.

Why 304 steel

Know what metal touches your face

Many cheap tools say "stainless" without naming the grade. We use 304 (18/8) stainless steel, the grade used for kitchen and medical equipment, and we publish the mill certificate for each batch. Steel holds cold, skips the pores where bacteria hide in stone, and survives a drop on a bathroom tile.

Questions

Before you buy

Does gua sha change my face shape?

No tool reshapes bone. Gua sha and cold rolling reduce morning puffiness and release facial tension, so the jaw and cheeks look more defined for a few hours. Daily use keeps that effect going.

How cold should I get it?

Ten minutes in the freezer or a few hours in the fridge. If it feels painful, let it sit at room temperature for a minute before use.

Steel or rose quartz?

Steel stays colder, is easier to sanitize and doesn't chip. Rose quartz feels heavier and warmer. Our quartz comes with an origin card so you know it isn't dyed glass.

Is the heated tool safe for sensitive skin?

It has three heat levels and a cold mode. Start on the lowest heat for two minutes, and skip it on broken or irritated skin.

Can men use these?

Yes. The Jawline Kit is the same 304 steel in a matte black finish, with a printed 5-minute guide focused on the jaw and under-eye area.
